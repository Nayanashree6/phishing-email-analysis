# Staff Phishing Awareness Guide

## STOP • CHECK • REPORT

### 1. Stop

* Do not rush when an email creates urgency.
* Never share passwords or verification codes through unsolicited email requests.
* Avoid unexpected attachments and suspicious links.

### 2. Check

* Examine the sender's actual email address.
* Look for spelling mistakes and unusual requests.
* Be cautious of unexpected prizes or payment requests.
* Verify unusual requests through a trusted communication channel.
* Remember that logos and familiar names can be copied.

### 3. Report

* Use your organization's approved phishing-reporting process.
* Preserve the original email when possible.
* Contact your IT or security team if you are unsure.
* Report immediately if you clicked a suspicious link or shared sensitive information.

### If You Made a Mistake

Do not panic. Report it promptly to IT or security and follow their instructions to protect your account and device.

### Remember

**Think before you click. Verify before you trust. Report when in doubt.**
